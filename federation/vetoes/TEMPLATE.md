## Triage

**Written by:** the accounts that wrote this triage. **Party to the contested decision:** none / who, and how.

Whoever decided cannot be the only one to examine their own decision. If everyone available is a party, say so here and leave the triage open.

### Verification

Which facts were checked, against what records or sources, and what was found.

### Interpretation

What the new information means for this decision, not only whether it is true.

### Detection

Error, misunderstanding or manipulation considered, including coordinated vetoes and fabricated grounds, and what was found.

### Proportion

Whether a bounded test or a temporary exception comes before a general change.

### Human recourse

Phase 0 has no council venue (DAF-001 §2). If the triage is contested, it stays open here until one exists.

## Outcome

**State:** open / adjustment / not passed

**Reasons.** Written so that whoever vetoed can see how their grounds were read. For an adjustment, which one: an exception, a narrower scope, a new test or the revision of the rule, and where it was recorded. A veto that does not pass stays in the record and can be reconsidered when new evidence appears.
